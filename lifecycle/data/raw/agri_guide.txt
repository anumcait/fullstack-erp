Agricultural Guide: Essential Farming Practices

1. Crop Seasons: Kharif vs Rabi
Agriculture in India is primarily divided into two main seasons: Kharif and Rabi.
- Kharif Crops: These are monsoon crops, sown at the beginning of the rainy season (June-July) and harvested in autumn (September-October). Major Kharif crops include rice, maize, sorghum, and cotton.
- Rabi Crops: These are winter crops, sown in the post-monsoon period (October-November) and harvested in spring (March-April). Major Rabi crops include wheat, barley, mustard, and gram.

2. Wheat Cultivation and Fertilizers
To improve wheat yield, balanced fertilization is crucial.
- Nitrogen (Urea): Essential for vegetative growth. Apply in split doses—half at sowing and the rest during irrigation.
- Phosphorus (DAP - Diammonium Phosphate): Important for root development and early growth. 
- Soil Testing: Farmers should always apply fertilizers based on soil test results to avoid nutrient imbalance.

3. Summer Crop Protection (30-45°C)
Summer heat can severely stress crops. Effective protection strategies include:
- Irrigation Timing: Irrigate during early morning or late evening to minimize water loss due to evaporation.
- Mulching: Use organic mulch (like straw) to cover the soil surface, which helps retain moisture and regulate soil temperature.
- Heat Stress Monitoring: Frequently check for signs of wilting and ensure consistent water supply.

4. Irrigation for Wheat
Wheat requires adequate moisture, especially during critical growth stages like crown root initiation and flowering.
- Frequency: Depending on soil type and weather, wheat should be irrigated every 15 to 20 days.
- Methods: Flood irrigation is common, but sprinkler irrigation is more efficient in water usage.

5. Monsoon Planting Advice
The monsoon season provides the necessary hydration for many crops but brings challenges:
- Drainage: Ensure proper field drainage to prevent waterlogging, which can cause root rot.
- Fungal Diseases: Increased humidity can lead to fungal infections. Monitor crops closely and use fungicides if necessary.
- Sowing: It is the ideal time for sowing Kharif crops like paddy (rice) once the rains are established.
