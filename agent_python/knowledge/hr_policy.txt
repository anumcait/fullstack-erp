# ERP HR POLICY - LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE

1. SICK LEAVE: Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days.
2. CASUAL LEAVE: 12 days of casual leave are provided. These must be applied for 2 days in advance.
3. WORKING HOURS: Official hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Grace period is 15 minutes.
4. LATE POLICY: Arriving after 9:15 AM more than 3 times a month will result in a half-day deduction.
5. OVERTIME: Overtime must be pre-approved by the department head.
